Know before you go

This app searches a persistent, regularly refreshed spatial database. OpenBeta provides real climbing places and OpenSkiData provides mapped ski areas worldwide; RIDB supplies US hiking, biking and backpacking activity records, with the curated seed covering additional areas. Data & refresh health shows current counts and source freshness. It is not an exhaustive inventory and missing records do not mean an activity is impossible. Areas within 450 km straight-line distance are candidates; only road-network estimates assign the two driving tiers, with areas beyond four modeled hours excluded. Road gateways are approximate selected access points, not verified open trailheads. Activity tags indicate regional opportunities, not current openings or a verified itinerary.

Weather colors

The worst daily result across the full trip determines the label. Missing trip data stays Unranked. Excellent means the forecast meets our simple weather preferences; it is not an assessment of outdoor safety.

For hiking, backpacking, biking and bikepacking: Excellent generally means highs 8–28°C, gusts at most 35 km/h, precipitation at most 3 mm/day and rain chance at most 45%. Poor includes highs below 0°C or above 35°C, gusts above 65 km/h, snowfall above 1 cm, or rain at least 15 mm (10 mm for biking/bikepacking). Intermediate conditions are Passable.

Climbing is more sensitive to rain: above 0.5 mm is Passable; 3 mm or more is Poor. Rock drying, ice and rock type are not assessed.

Ski weather: Excellent has highs −10 to 3°C, gusts at most 35 km/h, and snowfall at most 10 cm/day. Highs outside −18 to 8°C, gusts above 65 km/h, snowfall above 25 cm, or more than 8 mm of precipitation with highs above 1°C are Poor. Other conditions are Passable. Snow base, lifts, terrain, rain/snow transition and avalanche danger are not verified.

For camping, hourly temperatures from 8 pm to 7 am between each trip day are included. Below 7°C is Passable and below 0°C is Poor, regardless of camping equipment. Permission, seasonal access and permits must be checked separately. No campsite inventory is queried.

Map detail & saved trips

Zoom in to see nine weather samples in a 10-mile-radius circle. Every dot uses the same activity, dates, camping-night rules and color thresholds as the regional list. Today uses the full forecast day rather than only the hours remaining; these are forecasts, not real-time observations. These are forecast sample coordinates, not verified recreation sites, access points, trails, or campsites. Forecast models may be coarser than the spacing between dots.

The three suggested activity/area combinations use complete forecasts and modeled drives under four hours, one per larger region. Weather rating comes first, then shorter drive; your selected activity wins ties. Backcountry skiing is considered only when you select it. These are weather comparisons, not checks of skills, terrain or access.

Saved trips live only in this browser and retain their original dates. Forecasts are fetched again when reopened. Campsite searches open FreeCampsites.net at the selected coordinates. Its listings are not loaded or redistributed in this app; check each listing and the land manager separately.

Live sources

Open-Meteo forecast and geocoding, with location names from GeoNames. Data under CC BY 4.0. Free service is intended for noncommercial use.

OSRM road-network estimates to a regional gateway, using OpenStreetMap. No live traffic, weather, closures, ferry schedules or trailhead driving. These are approximate, not guaranteed arrival times. Service failure leaves drive time unknown.

Map tiles by OpenFreeMap using OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap data. Map labels follow the device/browser language, with transliterated names when a translation is unavailable. Area details link directly to land managers for access and camping. Area details link the relevant avalanche service when one is recorded. Avalanche-service coverage is regional. This app does not retrieve or interpret bulletins and never substitutes a Washington bulletin for another region.

Your origin is sent to the geocoding/routing providers when used; weather queries use destination points. Trip dates are interpreted in each destination’s local timezone. Trip settings and your selected origin are saved only in this browser. Device location requires your browser’s permission.

Data & refresh health

Nearby places come from the persistent location database. Climbing and skiing have broad international source coverage. RIDB adds US hiking, biking and backpacking areas; other activities retain the verified seed. Activity evidence, access and overnight permission are separate. We do not verify current openings, snow base or campsite inventory.
